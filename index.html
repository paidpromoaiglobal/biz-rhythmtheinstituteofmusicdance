About Us

Rhythm- The Institute Of Music & Dance

Rhythm – The Institute of Music & Dance is a premier arts academy located in the heart of Jawahar Nagar, Jaipur, dedicated to nurturing talent across all age groups. With a passionate team of highly experienced faculty, Rhythm offers training in Kathak, Bharatanatyam, Hip Hop, Freestyle Dance, and Music — both in-person and through interactive online sessions. Our institute is more than just a class; it is a platform that promotes positivity, creativity, and artistic growth.

At Rhythm, we believe that art has no age and no boundaries. Whether you are a young child discovering the joy of movement, a teenager looking to refine your skills, or an adult pursuing a lifelong passion, our professional yet friendly environment ensures every student feels encouraged and inspired. Our online classes are designed to be equally immersive and engaging, making quality arts education accessible from anywhere.

  • Highly experienced faculty for Kathak, Bharatanatyam, Hip Hop, Freestyle, and Music
  • Available in both offline (Jaipur) and interactive online modes
  • Welcoming environment for all ages — from kids to adults
